# 🏆 Asper Beauty: Customer Success Report

This report is the definitive **"Medical Record"** for your business. It turns
raw data into **Pharmacist Logic**, so you can assess the health of your
storefront with the same precision as a skin consultation—and monitor the pulse
of the **"Authentic Quality"** promise.

**Report title:** Asper Digital Pharmacy Rounds ("Concierge Log")\
**Frequency:** Weekly (recommended: same day each week). Copy the template below
into Google Docs, Notion, or a spreadsheet; fill one report per period.

---

## How this report serves the Asper DNA

**The conversion funnel: Concern → Cart**\
**Section II (3-Click Solution Performance)** is the core of the document. It
measures how well the Digital Concierge narrows 5,000 SKUs into a personalized
prescription.

- **Completion rate (> 40%):** If low, the "Morning Spa" experience may be too
  cluttered or the questions too complex.
- **Prescription conversion (> 15%):** Shows users trust the "Pharmacist's
  Stamp" enough to add the recommended Vichy or La Roche-Posay items to their
  tray.

**The trust index: Authenticity & COD**

- **Trust signal (Section V):** In the first month, a high count of "Is this
  authentic?" queries is a positive sign—it means the Authenticity Guard script
  is actively addressing the "fake product" fear in the market.
- **COD rate (> 70%):** In Jordan, a high Cash on Delivery rate is the strongest
  "Bridge of Trust" signal: customers feel safe to start a transaction and
  verify the Seal of Authenticity on delivery.
