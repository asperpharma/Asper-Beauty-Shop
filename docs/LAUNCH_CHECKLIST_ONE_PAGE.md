# 📋 Asper Beauty: "Go-Live" Protocol

**Domain:** asperbeauty.com | **Brand DNA:** Eternal Elegance

---

## 🚨 THE DOMAIN PULSE (Hour 0:00)

**Visual Check:** Open site on mobile. Is the background **Soft Ivory**
(`#F8F8FF`) (The Digital Tray)?\
**Security Check:** Confirm the **Green Padlock (SSL)** is active in the address
bar.\
**Branding Check:** Does the **Maroon** (`#800020`) logo (The Pharmacist's
Stamp) appear sharp against the Ivory?

- [ ] **Hour 0:00:** Open asperbeauty.com on your phone. Confirm Soft Ivory +
      SSL + Maroon logo. Full plan:
      [LAUNCH_DAY_PROTOCOL.md](LAUNCH_DAY_PROTOCOL.md).

---

## Phase 1: Visual DNA Audit

- [ ] **Typography:** Verify **Playfair Display** for headings (Luxury) and
      **Montserrat** for UI/Body (Science).
- [ ] **The Midas Touch:** Tap a product card. Does a **1px Shiny Gold**
      (`#C5A028`) border appear on interaction?
- [ ] **Photography:** Confirm product imagery uses high-key "Clinical
      Brightness" and morning sunlight.

---

## Phase 2: Operational "Pharmacist Logic"

- [ ] **Sales Intelligence:** Bot drives the 3-Click flow (Concern → Skin Type →
      Prescription → Cart). See
      [SALES_INTELLIGENCE_AND_PHARMACIST_PLAYBOOKS.md](SALES_INTELLIGENCE_AND_PHARMACIST_PLAYBOOKS.md).
- [ ] **The 3-Click Solution:** Test the concierge. Can you go from "Skin
      Concern" to "Cart" in 3 clicks?
- [ ] **Catalog Integrity:** Search for Vichy or La Roche-Posay. Confirm 5,000
      SKUs are correctly tagged.
- [ ] **Payment:** Ensure **Cash on Delivery (COD)** is the primary, trusted
      checkout option.

---

## Phase 3: Infrastructure & Trust

- [ ] **Store Details:** Address set to Jordan. Currency **JOD**. 2FA enabled
      for asperpharma.
- [ ] **Shipping:** Standard **3 JOD** / Free over **50 JOD**. Policies in
      _Settings > Legal_.
- [ ] **LIVE:** Remove Store Password. Test COD order → "Order Confirmed" email
      with Maroon branding.
