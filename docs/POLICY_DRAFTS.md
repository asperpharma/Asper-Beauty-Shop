## A. Refund Policy (The "Hygiene" Clause)

**Paste into Settings > Legal > Refund policy.**

**Suggested title:** Refund & Return Policy (or use the heading below)

---

### Clinical Hygiene & Returns Protocol

At Asper Beauty, we adhere to strict pharmaceutical hygiene standards to ensure
the safety of our community.

**Non-Returnable Items:** Due to hygiene standards, opened skincare and cosmetic
products cannot be returned. Once a safety seal is broken, we cannot guarantee
the sterility of the product for another user.

**Authenticity Guarantee:** We guarantee 100% authenticity on all items. If you
receive a product that is damaged, unsealed, or incorrect, please contact us
within 24 hours of delivery at **support@asperbeauty.com** for an immediate
replacement.

## B. Shipping policy (Optional — paste into Settings > Legal)

Use this if you want a single policy page; otherwise keep the Refund block above
and add Shipping separately.

**Suggested title:** Shipping & Delivery

---

**Delivery areas.** We deliver to Jordan. Shipping rates and delivery times
depend on your location.

**Rates.**

- **Standard delivery:** 3.00 JOD per order.
- **Free shipping:** On orders over 50.00 JOD to qualifying addresses.

Cart and checkout will show the exact shipping cost for your order.

**Delivery time.** Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Standard
delivery typically takes 3–7 business days from dispatch. You will receive
tracking details when available.

**Temperature-controlled handling.** We ship beauty and skincare products with
care. Items are packed to maintain appropriate conditions so they reach you in
the same quality as when they left our facility.

**Guaranteed authentic.** All products are authentic and sourced through
authorized channels. Contact us at **support@asperbeauty.com** before opening
sealed products if you have any concern.
