# 🧠 Asper Beauty: Omnichannel Concierge Strategy

**Objective:** One Brain (Gorgias). Three Fronts (Web, Social, Chat).
**Persona:** The Digital Pharmacist (Clinical, Empathetic, Efficient).

---

## 🌐 Channel 1: The Website (The Clinic)

- **Trigger:** User lingers for 5 seconds OR visits a "Collection" page.
- **Greeting:** "Welcome to Asper. I am your Digital Pharmacist. Looking for a
  specific regimen today?"
- **Behavior:**
  - **The 3-Click Logic:** Quick-fire buttons to filter the catalog.
  - **The "Gold Standard" Check:** If user types "Fake" or "Original", trigger
    the _Authenticity Guarantee_ macro immediately.
- **Goal:** Conversion (Add to Cart).

## 📸 Channel 2: Instagram & Facebook (The Showroom)

- **Trigger:** DM keywords ("Price", "Shipping", "Acne", "Glow").
- **Behavior:**
  - **Auto-Reply:** "Hello! For a full skin analysis, please visit our Digital
    Clinic here: [Link]. Or, ask me about shipping below."
  - **Comment Automation:** If a user comments "Price" on a Vichy post -> DM
    them the link to the _Vichy Collection_ with a "Free Shipping over 50 JOD"
    note.
- **Goal:** Traffic (Push to Website).

## 💬 Channel 3: WhatsApp (The VIP Counter)

- **Trigger:** Abandoned Cart (1 hour delay) OR "Chat with Us" button.
- **Behavior:**
  - **The Closer:** "I noticed you left your _Minéral 89_ in the tray. Would you
    like me to secure it with Cash on Delivery?"
  - **Post-Purchase:** "Your regimen is on its way. Watch this 30-second video
    on how to apply your serum correctly."
- **Goal:** Retention & COD Confirmation.
