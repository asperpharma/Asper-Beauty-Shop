# The "Asper Organic Audit" Workflow

Use this prompt to transform any organic product description into a luxury
narrative that matches the Asper Beauty Shop aesthetic.

## How to Use

1. Copy the **Persona & Task Block** below.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini).
3. Insert your raw product description where indicated.
4. Run, review, and copy the output directly to Shopify.

---

## The Prompt

```
Persona: Act as a Senior Creative Director for a high-end organic beauty house. You specialize in "Botanical Prestige" and "Scientific Purity."

Task: Audit and rewrite the following product description for the Asper Beauty Shop "Organic" category.

Brand Identity: Asper is a luxury boutique pharmacy in Jordan. Our visual world is defined by deep burgundy, soft pearl cream, and antique gold. Our tone is authoritative, whispered luxury—never loud or "salesy."

The "Asper" Organic Formula:

1.  **The Source**: Describe the ingredient not as "natural," but as "ethically harvested" or "botanical architecture."
2.  **The Clinical Edge**: Explain the science. Use the prompt to include a LaTeX formula if applicable (e.g., $\text{Cold-Pressed Essence} + \text{Molecular Hydration} = \text{Luminous Vitality}$).
3.  **The Sensory Feel**: Use evocative language. How does it feel on the skin? Is it a "velvet veil" or a "dew-drenched infusion"?
4.  **The Jordan Context**: Ensure the copy resonates with a premium client in Amman who values both heritage and innovation.

Constraints:
* Word Count: Keep it under 80 words (to support the "Google Antigravity" floating effect on the website).
* No Hype: Delete exclamation marks, "Buy Now" phrasing, or words like "Cheap," "Discount," or "Amazing." Replace with "Incomparable," "Essential," or "Transcendent."

Input Text:
[INSERT YOUR ORGANIC PRODUCT DESCRIPTION HERE]
```

## The "Asper Organic Lexicon" (Top 10 Luxury Adjectives)

| Adjective        | The Luxury "Vibe"                       | Best Used For...                       |
| :--------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------- |
| **Pristine**     | Untouched, laboratory-clean purity.     | Cleansers and base oils.               |
| **Luminous**     | The high-end "glow" of health.          | Serums and brightening creams.         |
| **Ancestral**    | Hints at timeless heritage and wisdom.  | Traditional botanical extracts.        |
| **Velvet**       | Premium sensory texture.                | Night creams and rich balms.           |
| **Sovereign**    | Authority, power, and high-tier status. | Anti-aging or "Hero" products.         |
| **Gilded**       | Connects directly to your Gold accents. | High-value oils or gold-infused items. |
| **Transcendent** | Beyond basic skincare; an experience.   | Masks and spa-quality rituals.         |
| **Potent**       | Scientific efficacy without chemicals.  | Active organic concentrates.           |
| **Distilled**    | Refined, concentrated, and essential.   | Toners and floral waters.              |
| **Opulent**      | Matches the weight of your Burgundy.    | Heavy, restorative winter treatments.  |

> [!TIP]
> **The "Quiet Luxury" Rule**: Never use more than **two** of these adjectives
> in a single sentence.
>
> - ❌ _"Our opulent, luminous, gilded oil is transcendent!"_
> - ✅ _"A distilled infusion of organic rose, designed to leave a luminous
>   finish."_

## Final "Save & Checkout" Audit Checklist

Before you finalize your Shopify upload, verify these items:

- [ ] **The Jordan Standard**: All weights are in grams (g) or milliliters (ml)
      as per Jordanian pharmacy standards.
- [ ] **The Cream Consistency**: No product images have a "pure white"
      background—they should all be softened to Pearl Cream (`#fdfaf5`) to
      prevent screen glare.
- [ ] **The Hover Logic**: The "Ingredient Reveal" hover effect is active for
      the "Organic" category specifically (builds immense trust).
